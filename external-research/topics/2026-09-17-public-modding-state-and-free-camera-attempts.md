# Heavy Rain modding in public: a Quantic Dream modding community, unpacked archives, and partial free cameras

**Status:** 🆕 new · **Priority:** low-medium — thin, but it saves re-discovery.

## What is public

- A **Quantic Dream Modding Discord** is where modders of all Quantic Dream games share methods
  `[reported]`.
- The PC files sit in the Steam or Epic install folder and use a compressed "segs" archive format that
  community tools unpack `[reported]`.
- **Free-camera attempts** follow Frans Bouma's (Otis_Inf) camera-hacking tutorial; modders describe
  Heavy Rain's camera system as complicated, with position freezing and XYZ changes working but
  rotation proving hard `[reported]`.
- Heavy Rain Wiki documents a "Debug Mode" page (not readable by automated fetch) `[reported, title only]`.
- vorpX has a Heavy Rain forum thread (cinema mode) `[reported]`.

## Why it matters here

1. The camera search in the board's `[FLAT]` row is known to be awkward: position is easy, rotation is
   not. Expect the view matrix to be assembled from more than one source `[hypothesis]`.
2. The modding community is the place to ask before spending static-analysis time.
